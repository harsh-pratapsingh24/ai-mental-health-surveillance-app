Informed Consent

AegisMind provides confidential mental health support and distress prediction.

• Anonymized Session
All data is processed under a random anonymous token without personal identity tracking.
• Private Text Processing
Your journals are evaluated locally and in memory. Raw journal text is never sold or retained.
• Emergency Escalation
If severe distress is detected, emergency helpline options and counselor support are provided.

Clinical Terms Explained

DPI (Distress Prediction Index)
A composite score from 0.0 to 1.0 reflecting your current emotional load. It combines your words, mood sliders, and voice clarity. Lower numbers mean higher calm and balance.
Trajectory Velocity (ΔDPI)
The rate and direction of emotional change across your recent check-ins. A steady or negative slope means improving calm, while a steep rise flags potential fatigue.
Pronoun Density
Psychological research shows that when feeling overwhelmed, people naturally use more first-person words ("I", "me", "my"). Tracking this provides an objective wellness signal.
Absolutist Words
All-or-nothing words like "always", "never", "nothing", or "completely". In cognitive psychology (CBT), identifying these patterns helps build flexible perspective.
Emotional Valence & Arousal
Valence measures how pleasant or heavy an emotion feels (1 to 5). Arousal measures your physiological energy level from calm (1) to restless (5).
